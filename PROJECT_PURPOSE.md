VetThe.App — Purpose & Value Proposition
=========================================

VetThe.App is an open, community-driven catalogue that standardizes identity-security facts about SaaS applications (SSO, SCIM, MFA, compliance, and pricing tiers). It helps security and IT teams quickly compare vendor identity features, reduce risk during procurement and auditing, and automate integration checks.

Value:
- Saves time by providing a single, validated source of truth for identity-related vendor features.
- Enables automated workflows via machine-readable exports (JSON/CSV) to integrate into procurement and security tooling.
- Increases trust with provenance metadata and verification badges to show evidence and authoritativeness.

Primary outcomes:
- Faster vendor evaluation and procurement decisions.
- Improved configuration and security posture across enterprise SaaS apps.
- A maintainable community process for keeping app data accurate and auditable.
